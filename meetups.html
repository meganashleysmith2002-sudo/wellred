About the venue ✦

Meet South Florida Sewing Studio

South Florida Sewing Studio is a sewing school and community space in Fort Lauderdale. They teach everything from beginner sewing to pattern making and swimwear design, and offer studio memberships if you want to keep making on your own.

It's a place built around learning something with your hands and meeting people while you do it, which is exactly what we wanted for October.
