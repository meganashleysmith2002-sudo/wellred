About the venue ✦

Meet Amelia's

Amelia's is an independent cafe and bookstore in Fort Lauderdale, formerly Pulp & Press. They do craft coffee, breakfast, all-day brunch and lunch, with shelves of carefully chosen books and a calendar full of book clubs, afternoon teas, and workshops.

It's the kind of place you can stop by for a coffee, stay for lunch, and leave with your next read, which is exactly why we wanted to host here.
