Our Partners

Every WellRed meetup happens at a local South Florida business. These are the ones who have hosted us so far. We're dedicated to introducing our readers to more local spots and encouraging everyone to shop local.

Our first partner ✦

Alivia Ryan Salon Co.

A small business salon in Plantation, and the host of our very first meetup on August 29. We talked My Brilliant Friend over drinks and mocktails, made bookmarks with watercolor pens, offered permanent jewelry, and the salon threw in a discount and a giveaway for everyone who came.

Twenty members showed up, a full room, and a night we're still talking about.
