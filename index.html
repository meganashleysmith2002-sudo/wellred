How it works ✦

Three steps, that's it

i

The club votes

We put up four books. Members vote on the site, and the winner is the month's read.

ii

Everybody reads

Buy through our Bookshop.org list and the money goes to independent bookstores instead of a corporation. We get 10% back and it goes straight into better events.

iii

We meet up

Twice a month at a local South Florida business. Book talk, a drink, and usually something to make with your hands.

Who we are ✦

Two best friends and a lot of opinions

We are Kaitlyn and Meg, both born and raised in Coral Springs and best friends since we were fifteen. Making friends in adulthood is really hard. Everyone's working, everyone's scattered, and there aren't a lot of third spaces. So instead of waiting for something to happen, we built it.

Every meetup happens at a local South Florida business, on purpose.
