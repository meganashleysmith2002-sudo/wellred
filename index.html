How it works ✦

Three steps, that's it

i

The club votes

We put up four books. Members vote on the site, and the winner is the month's read.

ii

Everybody reads

Buy through our Bookshop.org list and the money goes to independent bookstores instead of a corporation. We get 10% back and it goes straight into better events.

iii

We meet up

Twice a month at a local South Florida business. Book talk, a drink, and usually something to make with your hands.

Who we are ✦

Two best friends craving community

We're South Florida natives, born and raised, best friends since we were fifteen, and we love reading, talking about good books, and having a little drink. We felt South Florida was missing a sense of community and girlhood, so we put a book club, a social community, and a drink of choice all in one place.

We're also dedicated to supporting local businesses, so each month we meet up at a local South Florida spot. That includes independent, locally owned bookstores, which is why we've partnered with Bookshop.org through their affiliate program.

And if you're wondering how joining works, there's no membership, no commitment. Just show up to a meetup, or hop into our Fable club where we talk through the book. We just want to get girls together.
