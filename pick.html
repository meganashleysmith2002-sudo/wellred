the story

What the book is about

In a working-class neighborhood of Naples, two brilliant girls forge a friendship that is magnetic, competitive, and impossible to forget.

My Brilliant Friend follows Elena and Lila from childhood into adulthood as they test what loyalty, ambition, and self-invention can mean. Their bond is fierce and shifting — inspiration, rivalry, protection, and pain. It's the opening novel of Ferrante's celebrated Neapolitan quartet.

Come ready to talk about the choices that pull these two women together — and apart.
