the story

What the book is about

Dominic Salt and his three kids are the last people living on Shearwater, a tiny island near Antarctica. They're there to pack up a seed vault before the rising sea swallows the island.

Then a storm washes a woman up on their shore, barely alive. Her name is Rowan, and she isn't the only one keeping secrets. It's part mystery, part love story, set somewhere wild and beautiful, and it asks what you would do to protect the people you love.
